Featured Projects
01
Urban Sprawl Analysis – Collin County, TX (2015–2025)
ArcGIS Pro Landsat 8 Remote Sensing Change Detection
+

Problem

Quantify the pace and spatial extent of built-up land expansion in one of the fastest-growing counties in the U.S. over a decade.

Methods

Maximum likelihood supervised classification on Landsat 8 imagery (2015 & 2025); post-classification change detection; Kappa coefficient accuracy assessment.

Results

98.33% overall accuracy (Kappa 0.9769). Built-up land increased 109.2% over the study period, representing rapid suburban conversion.

Impact

Demonstrates remote sensing workflows applicable to growth management, infrastructure planning, and regional equity analysis in high-growth metros.

02
DCTA Bus Transit Accessibility Analysis – Denton County
ArcGIS Pro Near Tool Spatial Join Transit Equity ACS
+

Problem

Assess whether DCTA bus stops are equitably accessible across Denton County census tracts, especially for low-income and car-free households.

Methods

Near Tool to calculate distance from tract centroids to nearest stops; Spatial Join to link ACS variables (income, vehicle access, race/ethnicity); four-tier access classification.

Results

Identified significant access disparities by income quintile and vehicle ownership, with lower-income tracts disproportionately falling in the lowest accessibility tier.

Impact

Provides an evidence base for route prioritization and service expansion targeting underserved communities in the DCTA network.

03
Fireclay Village TOD Effectiveness Study – Salt Lake City
TOD Analysis Peer-Reviewed Mixed Methods Journal of Urbanism
+

Problem

Evaluate whether the Fireclay Village TOD in Salt Lake City delivered on its intended goals of density, walkability, and transit ridership.

Methods

Literature review, site analysis, and comparative assessment of TOD performance metrics against planning objectives; co-authored peer-reviewed study.

Results

Published findings in the Journal of Urbanism (January 2026), contributing original empirical analysis to the TOD effectiveness literature.

Impact

Informs planners and transit agencies on design and policy conditions under which TOD achieves sustainable mobility outcomes.

04
Accra International Trade Fair Redevelopment – Thesis
Critical Urban Theory Displacement Qualitative GIS Global South
+

Problem

How does neoliberal urban redevelopment in Accra reproduce displacement and accumulation by dispossession for market traders at the Trade Fair site?

Methods

Qualitative fieldwork; ground rent and dispossession framework (Harvey, López-Morales, Manning); spatial mapping of tenure and displacement patterns.

Results

Thesis documents how entrepreneurialist city governance undermines informal livelihoods while producing space for elite consumption in the Ghanaian capital.

Impact

Contributes to postcolonial urban theory; defended May 2026 as M.S. thesis at UNT under Dr. Waquar Ahmed.
